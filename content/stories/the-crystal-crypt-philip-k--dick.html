The last ship from Mars to Terra hums through the void, its passengers held in a quiet terror, the particular stillness of those who have left something burning behind them. Among them moves Ralf Erickson, a man whose sample case carries a weight no leather should hold, and whose companions, a woman and a younger man, do not meet his eyes until the Martian checkpoint has fallen away to nothing but a speck of light. They have done something on the planet below. Something the black-clad Leiters would kill to undo. On this last ship, one last gamble.
