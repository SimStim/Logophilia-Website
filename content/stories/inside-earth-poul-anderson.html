The biotechnicians had been thorough, giving him a face no mirror could make familiar: blunt ears grafted flat, a scalp of subject-race hair, skin stained to a leathery brown that would outlast him. Under the alien sun of a conquered world, Conrad Haugen wears his borrowed flesh like a uniform, every gesture rehearsed, every reflex a lie.

The empire that remade him does not want obedience from its colonies. It wants rebellion; a fire to burn away the ancient divisions that keep mankind divided against itself. Conrad’s mission is to join the underground, earn their trust, and deliver them to defeat. But trust has a weight he did not anticipate, and the woman who looks at him with love in her eyes is looking at a man who does not exist. The quiet machinery of empire grinds forward, and Conrad must choose which self to betray.
